[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3841677765]Progression: Vehicles[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Remixes Vanilla Vehicles Expanded's vehicles into a full progression on the Vehicles tab, from makeshift cars and dirt bikes to tanks, aircraft and land carrier platforms.
[*]Gives vehicles clearer names and descriptions, such as the makeshift car, the minivan and the freight truck.
[*]Moves the transport pod onto the Vehicles tab, after aircraft.
[*]Places Ocean Expansion's sailing research in the Neolithic and Medieval eras.
[*]With Engine Industries, ties its engines to vehicle research.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Vehicles adds up to [b]34 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork cuts that to [b]at most 19[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]Gangplank[/b] (Neolithic, with Ocean Expansion)
[*][b]Crow's nest[/b] and [b]Grappling launcher[/b] (Medieval, with Ocean Expansion)
[*][b]Cars[/b] (Industrial): makeshift car and passenger cars
[*][b]Motorbikes[/b] (Industrial): dirt bike and motorcycle
[*][b]Boats[/b] (Industrial): small boat and large boat
[*][b]Vans and buses[/b] (Industrial): minivan and van, plus passenger buses and the light utility jeep with their mods
[*][b]Armored cars[/b] (Industrial): armored car and armored personnel carrier
[*][b]Armed vehicles[/b] (Industrial): flame tank and rocket artillery
[*][b]Aircraft[/b] (Industrial): helicopter and fighter plane
[*][b]Drop pods[/b] (Industrial): rocket pod and siege pod
[*][b]Field carrier[/b] and [b]Light tank[/b] (Industrial)
[*][b]Heavy haulers[/b] (Industrial, with Heavy Truck or Snow Crawler): flatbed cargo truck and snowcrawler
[*][b]Heavy platform truck[/b] and [b]Land carrier platform[/b] (Industrial, with Technical Map Vehicles)
[*][b]Motorhome[/b] (Industrial, with Vehicle Interior Framework)
[*][b]Mobile platforms[/b] (Industrial, with Vehicle Map Framework): platform logistics and upgrades
[*][b]Advanced engines[/b] (Industrial, with Engine Industries): heavy and jet engines
[/list]
Everything else unlocks with the research it already builds on:
[list]
[*]Garage doors: [b]Automobiles[/b], plus Carpentry with ProgressionLESS: Furniture or the door project with Progression: Furniture
[*]Small truck: Vanilla Vehicles Expanded's pickup truck project, now [b]Trucks[/b]
[/list]
With Vanilla Vehicles Expanded - Tier 3, its projects are grouped too:
[list]
[*][b]Utility vehicles[/b]: off-road jeep, freight truck and refrigerated truck
[*][b]Military vehicles[/b]: assault carrier, armored scout, missile artillery truck, artillery tank and military helicopter
[*][b]Sports cars[/b]: vintage car and supercar
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Needs Vanilla Vehicles Expanded. Supports:
[list]
[*]Vanilla Vehicles Expanded - Tier 3
[*]Vehicle Map Framework, with Ocean Expansion
[*]Technical Map Vehicles
[*]Vehicle Interior Framework
[*]Heavy Truck
[*]Utility Vehicle
[*]Snow Crawler (Continued)
[*]Engine Industries
[*]HE-Ford B700 Bus '87, HE-Chevrolet Tahoe '07, HE-Ford F350 Ambulance '90 and HE-Ford Crown Victoria Police Interceptor '99
[*]GTek's Vehicles - 1998 Subaru Forester
[*]Garage Doors Expanded and Boathouse
[*]ProgressionLESS: Furniture and Progression: Furniture
[*]Node Research
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Vehicles for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
